# Babonk

A one-screen-wide pixel platformer down a vertical well. You fall, and you keep yourself up by bonking: press BONK in the air and you home in on the nearest foe below or beside you that nothing solid hides; with none in reach you dash the way you hold (straight down if you hold nothing). Hitting a foe, a wall, a floor or a crate bounces you off it and gives the bonk back, so a good fall never touches ground. Each foe or crate you break on the way adds one to the chain; bare walls and floors keep you up but add nothing. Landing on a foe from above stomps it the same way. Touching the ground also gives the bonk back, but ends the chain.

- A run is three wells of set depth, each longer and meaner. Reach the bottom of one and you rest: +1 HP, then the next. Clear the third and you've won.
- Four HP. Foes and spikes take one, with a moment of blinking after. At none the run ends.
- Foes: walkers pace their ledge; floaters drift home until you come near, then stop with a "!" and a chirp, glare, and chase you; spikers can't be stomped, only bonked.
- A kill freezes the action for a few frames while the foe flashes mint, then it bursts; the longer the chain, the longer the freeze.
- Breaking a foe knocks stars out of it, one more for each kill already in the chain (up to five); a crate gives one. They bounce, drift to you when you're close, and blink out after a few seconds, so a long chain showers you with them.
- Points for foes, crates and stars, a bonus for a chain of 3 or more when you land, and one for each HP left at the bottom of a well. A top-ten table with three initials.
- The screen is 320 × 180, shown in big square pixels at the largest whole scale that fits the window, in three colours: a deep blue-black ground, pale mint, and a mid green for danger and reward. They sit far apart in lightness (dark, middle, light), so foes and stars stand apart from the hero and the walls without relying on hue. A blocky arcade pixel font.
- Wells are stacked from hand-made chunks, picked by a seed, so every chunk is built to be passable whatever sits above and below it. Blocks are now and then cracked or chipped.
- The page opens on the title. The pause menu has Resume, Sound and End the run.
